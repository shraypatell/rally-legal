Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 9, 2026

Rally ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Rally mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (used for account creation and login)
  • First and last name
  • Username
  • Display name

Usage Data

  • Study timer sessions (start time, duration)
  • Class enrollments
  • Leaderboard rankings
  • Selected university location

Social Data

  • Friend connections
  • Chat messages sent within the app

Guest Users

If you use Rally as a guest, we store a temporary session locally on your device. No personal data is sent to our servers for guest sessions. Guest sessions expire at midnight.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core features (timer, leaderboards, classes, social)
  • To display your profile and study activity to friends and classmates (based on your privacy settings)
  • To calculate and display leaderboard rankings
  • To deliver chat messages between you and your friends
  • To process subscriptions and in-app purchases

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following cases:

  • Other users: Your display name, username, study time, and class membership may be visible to other Rally users depending on your privacy settings.
  • Service providers: We use Supabase for authentication and data storage, and Superwall for subscription management. These providers process data on our behalf under their respective privacy policies.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Your Privacy Controls

Within the app, you can control:

  • Whether your timer activity is public or private
  • Whether your location is visible to others
  • Whether your class enrollments are visible to others

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (TLS/SSL) and implement row-level security policies to protect your data at rest. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

Rally is designed for college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase - Authentication and database (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Superwall - Subscription management (superwall.com/privacy)
  • Apple - In-app purchases and App Store (apple.com/privacy)

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy within the app or via email.
