7. Location Features

Location access is optional. When you enable Location public in Rally's Settings and grant iOS While Using the App permission, Rally checks for a nearby supported campus study spot when you enter the App and periodically while it is open and no study timer is running. Rally does not request Always location access, monitor background geofences, or track your movements while the App is closed.

Verification checks whether you are near a supported building; floors are selected manually. Availability depends on supported sites, device permissions, location accuracy, and connectivity. Starting a timer does not wait for a new GPS check. If a verified campus spot is unavailable, you can use Home or Somewhere else, and Rally remembers your selected fallback.

A verified session location records the session's starting study place, not your continuous physical position. Rally does not continuously check that you remain there while the timer runs. A location shown beside another user's session is not a guarantee that they are still at that place.

Location sharing defaults off for accounts created after this default was introduced on September 9, 2026. Existing accounts retain their saved choices. Campus-map visibility requires both Location public and Timer public to be on and is subject to blocking controls. A Friends filter controls what you see, not who can see your own shared place. Home and Somewhere else are manual labels and never appear on the campus map, though they may appear with shared activity according to your privacy settings.

You can study with core timer and task features without location permission. Verified campus presence requires permission and a successful check. Turn Location public off in Profile → Settings to stop detection and remove campus-map presence. You can also revoke location permission in iOS Settings to stop future checks. Changing permission or visibility does not delete past session-location records.

Location detection is approximate and should not be relied on for navigation, safety, or emergencies. Attempting to defeat or spoof location detection in order to claim study time you did not earn is a violation of section 4. How we handle location data is described in our Privacy Policy.
