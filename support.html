Getting started

Rally is a study app for college students. Start a focus timer, earn Focus Points as you study, climb your school and global leaderboards, study together in group sessions, and keep a daily streak going. Create a free account with your email, pick your school, and press start.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Open the app, follow the onboarding, and sign up with your email address and a password. We'll send a confirmation email — tap the link to confirm, then sign in. You can also try the app as a guest without an account (guest sessions are temporary and reset daily).

I didn't get my confirmation email.

Check your spam or promotions folder. If it's still missing after a few minutes, email support@rallystudy.app from the address you signed up with and we'll confirm your account manually.

What is Rally Pro, and how do I subscribe?

Rally Pro is an optional subscription that multiplies the Focus Points you earn and unlocks premium themes, advanced analytics, and charity donations. You can subscribe from the Pro screen inside the app. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase, and the subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How do I restore a purchase I already made?

If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, open Profile → Rally Pro (or the Pro screen) and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription and any Focus Point purchases tied to your Apple ID will be restored. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple, not inside Rally. On your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Rally, or open the App Store, tap your profile photo, and choose Subscriptions. From there you can change plan or cancel. Rally Pro also has a "Manage or cancel subscription" button that links to this Apple screen directly. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the current period ends.

What are Focus Points?

Focus Points are Rally's in-app reward for studying. You earn them automatically as your timer runs, at a higher rate with Pro and in group sessions. Points power leaderboards, cosmetics, and charity donations. You can optionally buy Focus Point bundles as a one-time in-app purchase.

Why does Rally ask for my location, and can I say no?

Location lets Rally set your study location for you. We've mapped the study spots on your campus — when you arrive at one, Rally switches you to it automatically, so it's already right when you open the app and press start. It's also what keeps the leaderboards honest: you can't claim to be at the library from your couch.

Rally uses geofencing, which means your phone tells the app when you cross into or out of one of those mapped spots. It works in the background so your location is ready when you open the app. It doesn't follow you around, and it doesn't record anywhere else you go.

You can say no. If you decline location, you just pick "Home" or "Somewhere else" yourself and everything else works the same. To change the permission later, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Rally. Whether other users can see your location is a separate setting inside the app. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID, or go to Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases → Purchase History. Refund decisions are made by Apple.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, study sessions, messages, photos, friendships, and other personal data from our servers within 30 days. This cannot be undone.

How do I report a user or block someone?

Every user can report content and block other users from within the app. We review reports of objectionable content and abusive users and typically act within 24 hours by removing the content and ejecting the offending user. If you need to escalate something urgently, email support@rallystudy.app.

How do notifications work?

Rally can send you study reminders and alerts when friends start or finish a study session or send you a request. You can turn notifications on or off any time in the app, or in your device's Settings → Notifications → Rally.

Is my data private?

You control whether your study activity, location, and class enrollments are visible to others, in the app's privacy settings. We never sell your personal data and we don't carry advertising SDKs. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
