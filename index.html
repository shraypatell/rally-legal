Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

SSP1 Labs LLC, an Arizona limited liability company ("we", "our", or "us"), operates the Rally Study mobile application ("Rally" or "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (used for account creation and login)
  • First and last name
  • Username
  • Display name
  • Account identifier (a unique ID generated when you create your account, used internally to associate your data with your profile)

Profile Content

  • Profile photo (optional — only collected if you choose to upload one; stored on our servers and associated with your account)
  • Bio (optional)

Usage Data

  • Study timer sessions (start time, duration)
  • Class enrollments
  • Leaderboard rankings
  • Selected university

Location

  • Location is optional. If you grant location permission, Rally detects when you arrive at or leave one of the study locations we have mapped on your campus. This sets your study location for you, so it is already correct when you open the App and start a session, and it is what allows study locations to be verified rather than simply self-reported.
  • How it works. We use geofencing: your device notifies the App when you cross the boundary of one of those mapped study locations. This happens in the background, including when the App is closed. We do not receive a continuous stream of your position, and we do not build a record of your movements or of anywhere you go outside the mapped study locations.
  • What we store. We store which mapped study location you are currently at — for example, a particular library — or "Home" or "Somewhere else". We do not store raw coordinates and we do not keep a location history.
  • If you decline. If you do not grant location permission, or you revoke it later, you simply choose "Home" or "Somewhere else" yourself. Every other feature of the App works normally.
  • Turning it off. You can change or revoke location permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Rally on your device.
  • Whether your location is visible to other users is controlled separately, inside the App's privacy settings. It is not shared with other users unless you turn that setting on.

Social Data

  • Friend connections
  • Chat messages sent within the App

Purchase & Subscription Data

  • Records of in-app purchases you make (Rally Pro subscription and Focus Point top-ups), including subscription status, renewal dates, and non-personal transaction identifiers.
  • Payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never receive or store your credit card number or billing details. We use RevenueCat to manage and validate purchases on our behalf.

Diagnostic & Device Data

  • Crash reports and error diagnostics (collected via Sentry) to help us find and fix bugs. These may include device model, operating system version, and app state at the time of a crash. They are not used to identify you and they are not used for advertising.
  • Push notification token (only if you enable notifications) so we can send you study reminders and friend activity alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or in the App.

Guest Users

If you use Rally as a guest, we store a temporary session locally on your device. No personal data is sent to our servers for guest sessions. Guest sessions expire at midnight.

What we do not collect

We do not carry advertising SDKs, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not build advertising profiles. The only third parties that receive your data are the service providers listed in section 10, each of which processes it on our behalf to run the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's core features (timer, leaderboards, classes, social)
  • To display your profile and study activity to friends and classmates (based on your privacy settings)
  • To calculate and display leaderboard rankings
  • To deliver chat messages between you and your friends
  • To set your study location automatically and to verify that study sessions take place where they are reported, where you have granted location permission
  • To diagnose crashes and fix defects

3. Legal Bases for Processing

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your personal data on the following bases: to perform our contract with you (running your account and the App's core features); with your consent (location detection, push notifications, and optional profile content, each of which you can withdraw at any time); and for our legitimate interests in keeping the App secure, diagnosing defects, and preventing abuse and fraudulent study claims.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we have not sold or shared personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising in the preceding twelve months. We share data only in the following cases:

  • Other users: Your display name, username, study time, and class membership may be visible to other Rally users depending on your privacy settings. Your location is visible to other users only if you turn that setting on.
  • Service providers: We use Supabase (authentication and data storage), RevenueCat (in-app purchase management), and Sentry (crash diagnostics). They process data on our behalf under their respective privacy policies.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.
  • Business transfer: If SSP1 Labs LLC is acquired or its assets are transferred, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5. Your Privacy Controls

Within the App, you can control:

  • Whether your timer activity is public or private
  • Whether your location is visible to others
  • Whether your class enrollments are visible to others

At the device level, you can grant or revoke location and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Data Storage, Security, and International Transfers

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (TLS/SSL) and implement row-level security policies to protect your data at rest. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device.

We operate from the United States, and our service providers store and process data in the United States. If you use Rally from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed there, under safeguards such as the Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. Crash diagnostics are retained by Sentry on a rolling basis under its own retention schedule.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to obtain a copy in a portable format, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent you previously gave. If you are in California, you also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

You can delete your account and its data yourself at any time from the Profile screen. For anything else, email support@rallystudy.app and we will respond within 30 days at no charge. If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we have handled your data improperly, you may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

9. Children's Privacy

Rally is designed for college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, email support@rallystudy.app.

10. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase - Authentication and database (supabase.com/privacy)
  • RevenueCat - In-app purchase and subscription management (revenuecat.com/privacy)
  • Sentry - Crash and error diagnostics (sentry.io/privacy)
  • Apple - App Store, in-app purchases, and push notifications (apple.com/privacy)

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy within the App or via email, and by updating the effective date above.
